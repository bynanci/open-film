# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [繁體中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-TW.md) · [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

OpenFilm contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful community, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or experience.

## Expected conduct

Treat people with care. Give specific, constructive feedback about work; accept corrections; credit others; respect privacy; and make space for different levels of technical knowledge. Disagreement about design is welcome when it stays focused on evidence and behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, repeated disruption, and publication of another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply in project discussions, issues, pull requests and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through an available private contact or reporting channel. If none is available, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without naming people or sharing sensitive details. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality as far as possible, avoid conflicts of interest and consider context and impact before acting.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or exclude someone temporarily or permanently. They should explain the conduct involved and the expected change, and permit a confidential appeal to an uninvolved maintainer where possible. Repeated or severe harm can justify immediate removal.

This policy is informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
