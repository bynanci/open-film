# Code of Conduct

OpenFilm contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful community, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or experience.

## Expected conduct

Treat people with care. Give specific, constructive feedback about work; accept
corrections; credit others; respect privacy; and make space for different levels
of technical knowledge. Disagreement about design is welcome when it stays focused
on evidence and behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, repeated
disruption, and publication of another person's private information without
permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply in project discussions,
issues, pull requests, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through a private
contact or reporting channel when available. If no private channel is published,
open an issue requesting one without identifying the affected person or sharing
sensitive details. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality
as far as possible, avoid conflicts of interest, and consider the context and
impact before acting.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove inappropriate content, limit
participation, or exclude someone temporarily or permanently. They should explain
the conduct involved and the expected change, and permit a confidential appeal to
an uninvolved maintainer where possible. Repeated or severe harm can justify
immediate removal.

This policy is informed by the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
