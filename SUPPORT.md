## Find guidance

- Browse the [documentation site](https://bynanci.github.io/open-film/) or the [documentation map](docs/index.md).
- Check the [current roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [v1.0.0 readiness](docs/roadmap/v1-readiness.md) for known limits and unfinished platform work.
- For a reproducible defect, [open a bug report](https://github.com/bynanci/open-film/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). For a new capability, use the [feature request form](https://github.com/bynanci/open-film/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Protect private data

Projects and media may contain names, source paths, capture dates, GPS, transcripts and private footage. Do not attach original media, project databases or unredacted logs. Prefer generated fixtures. If a report needs media, share only files you own and are permitted to disclose; remove paths, GPS and credentials first.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. Community concerns follow the private reporting guidance in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Languages and expectations

You may write in English, Traditional Chinese or Japanese. The user-facing guides are available in these locales; detailed technical records are primarily in English. This is a community-maintained project with no guaranteed response time or paid support program.
